I built the MVP with Expo and Supabase: sign-in, input validation, a pgvector store for recommendations, and Edge Functions. It includes an on-device journal with encrypted backups, a colour mood pad, and a heatmap of muscles worked. I led architecture, design, and cross-platform implementation.
