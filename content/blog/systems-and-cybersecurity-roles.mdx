title: Why Systems and Cybersecurity Roles May Be a Better Fit for Me
summary: Why I'm now targeting junior-level Systems Software Engineer roles, with a focus on cybersecurity.
date: "2026-11-26"
tags: [engineering]
---

Most of my recent career research focused on the most visible titles in tech, such as AI Engineer. Those roles are generally senior-level, and I've come to recognize the gap between where I am today and where those titles sit. That realization pushed me to look more carefully at entry points that directly translate the skills I already have.

I recently completed a career assessment through the APT platform. I went in skeptical, since most resources I've used have been too generic, outdated, or disconnected from how the industry actually hires. This one gave me more specific direction than I expected, pointing me toward systems-level and cybersecurity-related roles.

After a few months of genuine reflection and questioning if the results were skewed or an echo chamber of my own thoughts, that recommendation helped me see something I had been missing: I had been chasing what was current and heavily hyped rather than what I genuinely enjoy. The problems that hold my attention are how software works at the foundation and how it can fail or be defended. Those are also the areas where my academic background is strongest and what matters at the core of becoming an adjunct instructor in Computer Science.

I'm not opposed to modern tooling. I've built a full-stack progressive web app for personal use and use AI tools in my development workflow. The productivity gains are substantial, but so is the responsibility to carefully review AI-generated code for missing requirements and incorrect results. I see that review discipline as an essential engineering skill, and I bring it to every project.

Writing code is also what I enjoy most. I like designing and building software myself, understanding exactly what each line does, and taking pride in the result. Tools help me move faster, but the craft of engineering is what keeps me motivated.

I'm now targeting junior-level Systems Software Engineer roles, with a focus on cybersecurity-related opportunities. If your team works on low-level systems, infrastructure, or security problems and values engineers who build carefully and verify thoroughly, I'd welcome the conversation.
